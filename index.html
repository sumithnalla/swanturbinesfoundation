Who We Are
Transparent Emergency Relief Led Action Focused Aid Donors Worldwide

With compassion at our core, we work for relief, education and health with people facing hardship.

Swan Turbines Foundation is a public charitable trust created to advance education, medical relief and support for people facing poverty and hardship. Our work is guided by dignity, practical care and long-term community benefit.
