Who we are

We believe giving is more than charity — it is a shared promise of humanity.

Helping people starts with understanding.

Swan Turbines Foundation is a public charitable trust established to create meaningful, lasting support for people and communities in need.

Its registered objects include relief of poverty, education, medical relief, food and financial support, healthcare, and assistance for vulnerable people.

The trust is registered as Document No. 204 of 2026, Book IV, at the Sub-Registrar Office, Kukatpally.

Registered purpose

Charitable action with a broad public benefit.

The deed authorises initiatives for education, healthcare, food and financial assistance, hospitals and health services, awareness programmes, and support for women, children, older people and persons with disabilities.
